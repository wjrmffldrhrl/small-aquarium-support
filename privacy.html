물고기 티어와 도감 발견 기록, 조개·진주·코인·보석, 판매 속도와 영구 배율, 생산 시각, 어부 제안과 판매·보석 구매 기록은 기기의 앱 저장 공간에 보관됩니다. 최근 정상 진행의 백업도 같은 기기에 저장합니다. 이 정보는 개발자에게 전송하지 않습니다. 앱 삭제 시 기기에 저장된 진행을 잃을 수 있으며, 앱 자체의 클라우드·기기 간 복구는 지원하지 않습니다.

Small Aquarium Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026.

Small Aquarium is a single-player iOS and Android fish-growing game. It has no accounts or ads. No developer analytics destination is configured in the current build, and analytics information is not transmitted externally. Optional gem purchases are processed by Apple or Google. The developer does not receive card numbers or payment passwords.

Local progress

Fish tiers, codex discoveries, shells, pearls, coins, gems, selling speed, permanent multipliers, production timestamps, fisherman offers, sale receipts and gem transaction identifiers are stored in the app’s local storage, together with a previous valid backup. They are not sent to the developer. Deleting the app can remove progress. The app does not provide cloud or cross-device recovery.

Personal information and support

A random installation ID, device test status, and existing analytics consent preference are stored on the device. Analytics consent defaults to off and an update preserves the existing preference. When consent is on, events and their event-time test status are stored locally and are not transmitted remotely. No events are recorded while consent is off. Withdrawing consent clears pending records.

The app does not request or collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier. In-game goods and coins cannot be cashed out, traded externally, or transferred between players.

If you email support, your email address and message are used to answer and investigate your request. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. Contact: wjrmffldrhrl@gmail.com.

You can delete local progress by deleting the app. Support messages are retained for as long as needed to handle the request. To request deletion of support information, contact the same email address.

Apple and this website

App Store, TestFlight and Google Play download, purchase, testing and diagnostic information is handled by each store under its policies and your settings. Verified transaction identifiers and grant records are stored on the device to prevent duplicate grants and are not sent to a developer server. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose technical access information is handled under GitHub’s policies. Email services may handle support messages under their own policies.
